CISM Practices

Nine self-checking activities that turn each lesson into management judgment. Sort, rank, calculate, and work through exam-style cases as the security manager, watch your concept mastery build, and finish with a report you can print or save as a PDF.

Activities: 9Each: 30 to 50 minDomains: All fourFormat: Self-checkingReports: Print or PDF

How practice works

Concept practice for every lesson

Stations

Each activity has three scored stations and a reflection: sorts, rankings, calculations, and exam-style cases.

Self-checking

Every answer is checked on demand with the manager’s reasoning. Retry any station as often as needed.

Concept mastery

A mastery meter tracks each concept as you work, and the report recommends what to review next.

Summary report

Print or save the report as a PDF to submit it, with scores, missed items, reflections, and a signature line.

For faculty

Using the practices in class

  • As submissions: ask learners to print the summary report to PDF and upload it. Reports show scores by station, attempts, missed items with the correct reasoning, reflections, and time spent.
  • Placement: assign each activity after the lesson reading it reinforces. Each card links to that lesson section.
  • In class: run a station as a think-pair-share, then let pairs check answers and compare the manager’s reasoning.
  • Team mode: a pair or team can work one activity together and list every member on the report.
